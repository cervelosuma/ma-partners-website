M.A Partners / Atsushi Sugita

CEO

Atsushi Sugita杉田 篤

CEO, M.A Partners Co., Ltd.

Profile

Over many years, he has worked across a wide range of areas in the apparel and retail industries in Japan and overseas, including product development, production, materials and raw material sourcing, and supply chain development. He has also been involved in overseas business operations and management across multiple companies.

Drawing on his experience in operating companies, he founded M.A Partners in 2024. Today, he supports corporate management, business development, and supply chain building, mainly in the apparel and textile sectors.

Message

As industries and markets evolve, companies need flexible judgment and steady execution. M.A Partners brings together perspectives from products, distribution, sales, and management to engage with each client’s business. Our role is to clarify challenges, uncover possibilities, and choose a path forward together.
